## Metascience model (community.js)

1. **The live model is not Aydin's model.** `community.js` implements the Bala and Goyal (1998) / Zollman (2007) model (two actions, Beta beliefs over both, agents take their best guess and pool evidence with neighbors). Mohseni and Williams (2021), "Truth and Conformity on Networks," is a different model (agents make public statements, weighing accuracy against conformity; learn from private evidence and others' statements). "How it works" describes the two separately and cites the paper for its own finding (from the Springer abstract: networks that sustain diverse opinion, the less centralized and less connected ones, do best). If Aydin wants the strand's model to be his own, a conformity toggle would need his model's specification; not attempted.
2. **Parameters are this page's choices, not Zollman's:** 12 agents, chances 0.5 and 0.55, 5 tries per round, Beta parameters uniform in (0, 4], settled after 20 unanimous rounds, small world = cycle plus 2 random chords between agents at least 3 steps apart. The text says so. With seed 2007 and 500 runs each: cycle 99.4% better action (mean 58 rounds), small world 99.6% (48), complete 84.8% (6), sharing with everyone 83.8% (6). The cycle and small world do not differ in reliability at these settings; only cycle vs complete is claimed. These figures are hard-coded in the "How it works" text (`data-model-stat="results"`); rerun `node tools/test-models-community.mjs` and update the text if parameters change.
3. **Lock-in is not strictly absorbing** in this two-unknown-actions version: an agent whose estimate of A drifts below its frozen estimate of B retries B. Over 2000 further rounds, about 16% of settled runs briefly leave unanimity and 0 to 1% change outcome. The text says the community "stays" with the worse action and gives the exception in parentheses.
4. **Citation data for content workstream.** Crossref: Mohseni, A. and Williams, C. R. (2021) "Truth and Conformity on Networks," *Erkenntnis* 86(6), 1509-1530, doi 10.1007/s10670-019-00167-6, online 2019-10-26 (the old research page's recommended citation still says "Unpublished manuscript"; the CV agrees with Crossref). The old models page titles the Shiny app "Bayesian Learning with Conformity on Social Networks" (app https://amohseni.shinyapps.io/Truth-and-Conformity-on-Networks/, code https://github.com/amohseni/Truth-and-Conformity-on-Networks). Zollman (2007) *Philosophy of Science* 74(5) 574-587, doi 10.1086/525605; Bala and Goyal (1998) *Review of Economic Studies* 65(3) 595-621, doi 10.1111/1467-937X.00059 (both Crossref).
5. **No moving red light** in this model: red is reserved for lock-in on the worse action, so a red light would read as a lock-in signal. Evidence flow is shown by a slight brightening of the edges at each round. Design may want to revisit.
6. **Lane note.** `tools/test-models-community.mjs` sits in `tools/` (Infrastructure's directory) because this workstream's task assigned it there; `src/lab/community.njk` is a development page with `noindex`.
7. **Credence shown by tone** is P(chance of B > chance of A) under the agent's Betas, computed with a normal approximation to the difference (stated in the text). The agent's action uses the Beta means, so a node near 0.5 tone can sit on either side of its choice.
